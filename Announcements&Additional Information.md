## VOID Autumn Cup 2026

## Event Dates
- **Online Play‑In**: October 11 ‑ October 12, 2026 

- **LAN Main Event**: October 20 ‑ October 24, 2026 

- **Tournament Operator**: MACE MEDIA
- **Venue**: Shanghai, China, MACE MEDIA STUDIO CENTER
- **Event type**: Tier 2, open‑registration tournament (open to teams from all global regions) 

- **Registration Fee**: **$900 USD** 

- **Registration Window**: September 27 ‑ October 3, 2026 
-  The registration link will be published on September 27th via MACE MEDIA Github and official social‑media channels.

- **Team Cap**: Maximum of 16 registered teams 
- A total of 16 teams will be accepted through open registration. Registration is only valid once the payment has been completed.

- Initial seeding will be determined according to the official October 2026 Global VRS(If not be updated, the official September 2026 Global VRS will be used.).

## Supplementary Provisions
1. If more than 8 but fewer than 16 teams register, vacant bracket slots will receive byes in the Play‑In stage.

2. If exactly 8 teams register, the Play‑In stage will be cancelled.

3. If fewer than 8 teams remain registered , the event will be cancelled for failing to meet minimum competition requirements. Full refunds of registration fees will be issued to all registered teams.

4. Mace Media will not cover travel costs for participating teams. All travel‑related expenses shall be borne solely by each team.

5. During the LAN event, the organizer will provide accommodation for participating teams.Although no dedicated practice rooms will be available,the accommodation we provide will be equipped with training PC.

6. All tournament‑related communications will be sent exclusively to the email address provided on the registration form、

7. If a team fails to complete payment within 48 hours of submitting its registration, its entry will be given to the next team in the waiting list, ranked by registration time.

8. Re-fund policy: If your team needs to withdraw from the tournament before October 6, 2026(Beijing Time) we will try to replace your team with teams on the waiting list. If we find a replacement we will re-fund the full amount. If teams withdraw or fail to lock in lineup after October 6, 2026(Beijing Time) no refund will be made.

9. The event roster will be locked on October 7, 2026. No withdrawals are permitted after the Roster Lock deadline. Any registered team failing to play its scheduled matches will be recorded as a forfeit (FF).

## Play‑In (Online,)
- 16‑team single‑elimination bracket; all matches are Best‑of‑3 (BO3),the final 8 teams enter the playoffs.
- Server Nodes: Beijing, Chengdu, Hangzhou, Shenzhen, Hong Kong China,Singapore
